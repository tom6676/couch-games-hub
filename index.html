Important Buyer Safety Guide (Keys vs. Accounts)

Third-party marketplaces list products from multiple independent vendors:

  • Digital Activation Keys (Recommended): You receive a code that you redeem directly on your personal Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo account. The game belongs permanently to you.
  • Account Purchases: If a vendor sells a "Steam/PSN Account", you will receive login credentials. Safety Rule: If you choose an account, immediately change its password, associated email address, and activate two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Always check the seller's positive feedback percentage (>98% recommended) before finalizing your purchase.
